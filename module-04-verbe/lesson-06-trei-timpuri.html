Checkpoint: a parable in three tenses

Two verses about a house and a storm, with every tense from this week in them.

25 minAudio optionalCheckpoint

The wise builder, from Matthew 7:24–25. Read it twice. On the second pass, sort every verb into present, past or future.

Read it

Check yourself

Present: aude, hears; face, does. Future: voi asemăna, I will liken. Completed past: și-a zidit, a dat, au venit, au suflat, au bătut, nu s-a prăbușit. Ongoing past: avea, had.

Therefore whoever hears these words of Mine and does them, I will liken to a wise man who built his house on the rock. The rain fell, the floods came, the winds blew and beat against that house, but it did not collapse, because it had its foundation built on the rock.

What you can do now

  • Tell who is acting from a verb's ending.
  • Recognise past, present and future at a glance.
  • Hear să and know an invitation, a wish or a purpose is coming.
  • Tell a command to one person from a command to the room.

The house on the rock survives four verbs of weather. The house on the sand, two verses later, gets the same four verbs and a worse ending.
