The future, two ways

One future for the Bible and one for conversation. A sermon uses both.

25 minNo audio neededDrill inside

Promises are made in the future tense, and Scripture is full of promises. Romanian has more than one way to make them.

The written future

A helper plus the dictionary form, without its a. It is the formal, written future, and it is the one in your Bible.

WhoHelperExampleMeaning
Ivoivoi locuiI will dwell
youveivei vedeayou will see
he, shevava pieriit will perish
wevomvom cântawe will sing
you allvețiveți fiyou will be
theyvorvor moștenithey will inherit

The helpers are the standard set.

One spelling, two words. voi is the helper “I will”. It is also the pronoun “you all”. If a verb follows it, it is the helper.

The spoken future

In conversation the default is o să plus the să form: o să merg, I'm going to go. The o never changes. When the preacher leaves the text and speaks freely, expect this one.

Reflexives and negatives

The little pronoun goes in front of the helper: mă voi scula, I will arise. So does nu: nu voi duce lipsă, I will not lack.

The Bible says voi merge. Your neighbour says o să merg. Both of them arrive at the same time.
