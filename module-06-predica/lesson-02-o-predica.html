Reading a sermon transcript

A short sermon on a verse you know, built from the signposts you learned yesterday.

25 minAudio optionalReader

This is a practice sermon written for the course, nine sentences on Psalm 23:1. It is deliberately simple. Its job is to let you read sermon-shaped Romanian before you have to hear it.

Read it

Read it through once. Then find the five signposts.

Open the sermon in the reader

Check yourself

The signposts: Ce înseamnă aceasta? · În primul rând · Cu alte cuvinte · În al doilea rând · De aceea · În încheiere. That is six; one was a bonus.

The argument: David says “my Shepherd”, so God is near; the shepherd knows and cares for his sheep, so we are not afraid; read the psalm each morning this week.

Using your own sermons

You mentioned pulling a few sermons from the church's stream. The reader can take them. For each one, the course needs two things:

  • A transcript, as plain text. Automatic captions are a fine starting point. English stretches can stay in.
  • The audio, if you want to listen along, as a file next to the course.

Send a transcript back to the tutor and it comes back as a reader passage with every Romanian word glossed. The format is one entry in assets/passages.js: a title, a reference, and the lines.

Why transcripts first. A sermon you have read is a sermon you can follow. Lessons 6.3 and 6.4 depend on having at least one real sermon in this form, and they work with the practice sermon until you do.

Automatic captions will render Duhul Sfânt in several creative ways. Consider it a second comprehension exercise.
