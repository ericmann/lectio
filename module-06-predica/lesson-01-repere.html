How a sermon is built: the signpost phrases

A sermon announces its own structure. Learn the announcements and you always know where you are.

25 minNo audio neededDrill inside

Reading gave you the vocabulary. Listening is faster and nobody pauses, so this week you learn the phrases that tell you where in the sermon you are even when the sentence in between got away.

Everything in this lesson is the author's own selection of common sermon phrasing. Treat it as a starting list, and add what your own pastor actually says.

Appeal

Vă îndemn să …I urge you to …
Să nu uităm că …Let us not forget that …
Dumnezeu să ne ajute.May God help us.
Dumnezeu să vă binecuvânteze.May God bless you.

When the pastor switches to English. Yours sometimes does. Treat each English stretch as a rest stop: it tells you the topic for free, and you pick the Romanian back up at the next signpost.

În încheiere means “in closing”. As in every language, it is a statement of intent and carries no guarantee.
