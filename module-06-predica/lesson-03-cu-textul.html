Listening with the text in front of you

The bridge from eye to ear: hear the words while you can still see them.

25 minAudio neededPractice

This is the first lesson that needs sound. Use headphones, and pick a time when you can.

Reading and listening are different skills that share a vocabulary. The method here is the one language teachers call reading while listening, and the steps are the author's own version of it.

The loop

Do this with one passage in the reader. Start with Tatăl nostru, which you know cold, then Psalmul 23, then the practice sermon.

About the voice. With the files you render from ElevenLabs, the audio is clear and steady. Without them, the device voice is flatter and may not exist on every device. Either way it is cleaner and slower than a person in a room, which makes it the right first step and the wrong last one.

What to listen for

  • The whispered i. ești, veci, frați. One syllable each. Your eye still wants two.
  • Words running together. dă-ne-o is one burst. So is n-a.
  • Stress. Check it against the guesses you made in week one. This is where they get corrected.

With a real sermon

When you have a sermon from your church with its transcript, do the same loop a paragraph at a time: read the paragraph, play that stretch, replay it looking away.

The third play, eyes closed, is where you find out which words you were reading and which you were hearing. The numbers are usually humbling for about a week.
