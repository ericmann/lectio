Week 6 · Lesson 4

Listening with the text put away

Five minutes of a real sermon, no transcript, and a count of what you catch.

25 minAudio neededPractice

The goal was never to understand every word. It is to follow, and following starts with catching the words you know as they go past.

The exercise

  • Open a recent service from your church. It streams on YouTube.
  • Find the start of the sermon and play five minutes at normal speed. If it is too fast, YouTube's settings will slow it to 0.75.
  • Don't pause. Each time you hear one of the words below, tap it.

Words caught

Read your tally

  • Lots of și and Dumnezeu, little else. Normal for a first try. Your ear is finding word boundaries, and that is the hard part.
  • Signposts registering. If you tapped în primul rând or de aceea, you were following the structure.
  • Under twenty in total. Slow the playback and go again with the same five minutes. Repeats are allowed and useful.

English counts as a clue. When the pastor switches languages, use the English to predict the next Romanian sentence. Predicting is most of what fluent listeners do.

Do it again tomorrow

Same five minutes. The count goes up faster than you expect, because the second time you know what is coming.

și will win by a wide margin. It always does. The contest is for second place.
