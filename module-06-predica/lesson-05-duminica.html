Capstone: one full sermon, and your notes sheet for Sunday

The objective, attempted. Then a sheet you keep filling in every week after.

A SundayIn personCapstone

Six weeks ago the objective was one sentence: follow a sermon at church by spring. This is the lesson where you try.

Before Sunday

During the sermon

Don't translate. Listen for three things, in this order:

  • The text. The chapter and verse, said with cu between them.
  • The signposts. Each în primul rând or în al doilea rând is a point. Write a number when you hear one.
  • One sentence per point. Whatever you caught, in whichever language it arrives.

What “following” means. If you leave knowing the text, how many points there were, and roughly what each was about, you followed the sermon. Word-for-word comes later and is a different goal.

Afterwards

Fill this in the same day. It stays in this browser, and each Sunday adds to the list.

What comes next

  • Keep the sheet going. The words-to-look-up column is your vocabulary list from now on.
  • Feed the reader. Each sermon transcript you add becomes a passage with every word glossed.
  • Phase two. The plan after this is technical Romanian, built on your own writing.

The first Sunday you laugh at the pastor's joke at the same moment as everyone else, the course is over. Nobody will notice but you.
