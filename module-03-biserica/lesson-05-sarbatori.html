The greetings

  • Crăciun fericit! Merry Christmas.
  • La mulți ani! Literally “to many years”. It serves for New Year and for birthdays.
  • Hristos a înviat! Christ is risen. The answer is Adevărat a înviat!, He is risen indeed.

You already know the grammar. a înviat is the same past form as a iubit and a dat in John 3:16: the helper a plus a word ending in -t. Week four makes this a rule.

La mulți ani covers birthdays, New Year, name days and most toasts. It is the most reusable sentence in the language.
