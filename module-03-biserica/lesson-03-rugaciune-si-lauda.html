Prayer, praise, and the order of a service

What each part of the service is called, and the short instructions said between them.

25 minNo audio neededVocabulary

A service has a shape, and the shape has names. Knowing where you are in it is half of following along.

The parts

Guess, then tap

The instructions

These are the sentences said between the parts. They are the author's own selection of common phrasing, so listen for your church's versions.

You hearIt means
Să ne rugăm.Let us pray.
Să ne ridicăm în picioare.Let us stand.
Puteți lua loc.You may be seated.
Să cântăm împreună.Let us sing together.
Să deschidem Bibliile la …Let us open our Bibles to …
Să citim împreună.Let us read together.

One word starts them all. Să plus a verb ending in -m is “let us”. You don't need the verb to know you are being asked to do something.

What the congregation says

Amin! needs no translation. Aleluia! doesn't either. Slavă Domnului! is “Glory to the Lord!”. In the author's experience Romanian evangelicals also greet one another with Pace!, peace.

Puteți lua loc is the phrase to learn first. Standing when everyone stands is easy. Knowing when to sit is the test.
