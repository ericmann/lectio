Checkpoint: a Sunday welcome, line by line

Six sentences of the kind that open a service. Read them the way you will soon hear them.

25 minAudio optionalCheckpoint

This welcome was written for the course. It is practice text, and it uses only words from weeks one to three.

Read it

Check yourself

Peace to you, brothers and sisters! Welcome to the house of the Lord. We are glad that you are here this morning. Let us stand and let us pray. Then we will sing together and read from the Word of God. May God bless you!

The “of” constructions are casa Domnului and Cuvântul lui Dumnezeu.

What you can do now

  • Recognise the names and titles of God on sight.
  • Sort the core vocabulary of the faith into word families.
  • Tell which part of a service you are in.
  • Read a short welcome with nothing left dotted.

Bine ați venit is literally “well have you come”. Romanian compliments your arrival before you have done anything.
