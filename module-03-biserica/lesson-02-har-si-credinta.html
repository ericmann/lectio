Grace, faith, sin, forgiveness, salvation

The abstract nouns of the faith, and a trick for learning them three at a time.

25 minNo audio neededVocabulary

Yesterday was who. Today is what: the things God gives and the things people do about it.

The core words

Guess, then tap

Several are already in passages you know: har and adevăr in John 1:14, credința, nădejdea și dragostea in 1 Corinthians 13:13, neprihănire and milă in the Beatitudes, sufletul in Psalm 23.

A Cornilescu word. neprihănire is this translation's word for righteousness. Newer translations often say dreptate, and a preacher may use both.

Learn them in families

Romanian builds a verb, a noun and a person from one root. Learn the root and you get three words.

To do itThe thingThe person or quality
a crede, to believecredință, faithcredincios, faithful, a believer
a ierta, to forgiveiertare, forgivenessiertat, forgiven
a mântui, to savemântuire, salvationMântuitor, Savior
a păcătui, to sinpăcat, sinpăcătos, sinner
a iubi, to loveiubire, loveiubit, beloved
a se ruga, to prayrugăciune, prayer

The ending -re turns a verb into its noun: a ierta → iertare. You will meet it constantly.

Romanian has two words for love, dragoste and iubire. The Greek of the New Testament has more than that, and your preacher will mention this at least once a year.
