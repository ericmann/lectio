Fericirile: the Beatitudes

Eight lines built on one frame. Learn the frame and the middle is all that changes.

25 minAudio optionalReader

The Beatitudes are the kindest text in the course to a learner: every line is Ferice de cei …, căci ei vor …. Blessed are those who …, for they will ….

Before you read

Guess each word, then tap. These are the ones most likely to stop you.

Guess, then tap

Read it

Open the passage, read it through once without tapping anything, and then go back for the words you missed. Mark what you know as you go.

Open the passage in the reader

Adjectives as people. cei săraci, cei blânzi, cei milostivi: cei plus an adjective makes “the poor”, “the meek”, “the merciful”.

Eight blessings, one grammatical frame. It is the only passage in the Bible that works as a substitution drill.

Drill

Answer from memory after you have read the passage.
