The prodigal son

A story this time, from Luke 15. The reader has four verses: the opening, the son's decision, the father's welcome and the father's explanation.

Open the passage in the reader

Watch the tense change in verse 24. era mort, era pierdut: the state he was in. a înviat, a fost găsit: the event that ended it. The grammar tells the story.

a căzut pe grumazul lui is “fell on his neck”. It reads better in the original than it sounds in any translation.
