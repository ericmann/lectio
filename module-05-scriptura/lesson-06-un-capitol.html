Week 5 · Lesson 6

Checkpoint: a chapter, unassisted

No glosses, no reveals. A real chapter on a real Bible site, and a count of what stops you.

25 minNo audio neededCheckpoint

Everything so far has had a safety net. Today there isn't one, and this is the last step before sermons.

The task

  • Open John 1 in the Cornilescu translation. You have read verses 1–5 and 14 with help. Read verses 1–18 straight through.
  • Don't look anything up on the first pass. Keep moving when a word stops you.
  • On the second pass, write down the words that stopped you. Ten or fewer in eighteen verses is a pass.

What to expect. The names will be easy: Ioan, Moise, Isus Hristos. The connectives and the verb endings are yours. What stops you will be individual nouns and verbs, and that is the normal state of reading in a second language.

Before you start

Tick these off honestly. Any you can't tick is a lesson worth one more visit.

Then come back

What you can do now

  • Read familiar Scripture in Romanian at something close to reading speed.
  • Keep going past an unknown word.
  • Use a real Romanian Bible site without help.

Somewhere in verse 15 John the Baptist says a sentence that is confusing in every language. That one doesn't count against your ten.
