1 Corinteni 13

The love chapter: a long run of present-tense verbs with a single subject.

25 minAudio optionalReader

You read verse 7 in week four. Today the passage around it, from 1 Corinthians 13. Nearly every verb is in the “he, she, it” form, because nearly every verb is about dragostea.

Before you read

Guess each word, then tap. These are the ones most likely to stop you.

Read it

Open the passage, read it through once without tapping anything, and then go back for the words you missed. Mark what you know as you go.

Open the passage in the reader

Reflexives everywhere. se laudă, se umflă, se poartă, se mânie, se gândește, se bucură. Six in three verses. The se is part of the verb; read through it.

se umflă de mândrie is “swells up with pride”. It is the same verb Romanians use for a tyre.
