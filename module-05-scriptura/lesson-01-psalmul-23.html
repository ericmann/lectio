Psalmul 23

Six verses you know by heart in English, now in the language you will hear them in.

25 minAudio optionalReader

This week changes the method. You already know what these passages say, so the Romanian has to be the thing you are learning. Start with Psalm 23.

Before you read

Guess each word, then tap. These are the ones most likely to stop you.

Guess, then tap

Read it

Open the passage, read it through once without tapping anything, and then go back for the words you missed. Mark what you know as you go.

Open the passage in the reader

Why the endings pile up. valea umbrei morții is three nouns: the valley, of the shadow, of death. Read it from the left and peel one ending at a time.

untdelemn is the word for oil here. It is built from “butter of wood”. Olive growers were not consulted.

Drill

Answer from memory after you have read the passage.
