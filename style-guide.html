Style guide

Every colour, typeface and component lives in assets/theme.css. Change it there and every page follows.

Identity

Lectio, Latin for “a reading”. The look is a psalter: parchment, ink, the blue of the Voroneț frescoes, and rubric red for the letters that matter.

Tone: light, dry, brief. One small joke per lesson, never at the reader's expense. The sacred texts are treated plainly and with respect.

Objective: follow a sermon at church by spring.

Body prose is Literata at a generous line height, capped at about 66 characters a line. Paragraphs stay under five lines.

Romanian is always set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a face designed so similar letters stay distinct, and always a step larger than the English around it: Mulțumesc, și vă rog să citiți încă o dată.

Device and accessibility choices

  • Desktop first, checked at 375, 768 and 1280 pixels wide. Tap targets are at least 44 pixels.
  • The red on marked letters is an aid, never the only signal; the mark itself is always there, and the highlight can be switched off.
  • Audio is optional everywhere. It uses the device's own Romanian voice when one exists and says so when it doesn't.
  • Reduced-motion settings are respected. Everything is reachable by keyboard.

Don't

  • Don't set Romanian in the body serif or at body size.
  • Don't use red for anything except the five letters, the eyebrow labels and errors.
  • Don't state a fact about the language without a link, or mark it as experience.
  • Don't put colours, fonts or sizes in a page. They belong in the theme.
