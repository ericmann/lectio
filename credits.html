Credits and sources

Everything this course depends on, and every source its lessons cite.

Typefaces

  • Literata · TypeTogether for Google · fonts.google.com/specimen/Literata · SIL Open Font License. Used for all English prose and headings.
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible Next · Braille Institute of America · fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible+Next · SIL Open Font License. Used for every Romanian word.

Libraries

None. The pages use plain HTML, one stylesheet and two small scripts, with no third-party code.

Audio

By default the Hear buttons use the Romanian voice built into your device. If pre-rendered files are present in the audio folder, they were generated from the course text with ElevenLabs text to speech by the course owner, and they are synthetic speech.

Scripture

Bible text is from Dumitru Cornilescu's Romanian translation, first published 1921–24, shown in current spelling and linked at the point of use. Each quoted verse was checked against the linked page when the course was built.

Practice texts

The Sunday bulletin, the welcome and the short sermon in the reader were written for this course by the author. No church published them.
