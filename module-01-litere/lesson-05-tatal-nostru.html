Week 1 · Lesson 5

Checkpoint: Tatăl nostru, start to finish

The whole prayer, aloud, with every rule from this week in play.

25 minAudio optionalCheckpoint

Five days ago this was a wall of marks. Today you read it aloud, start to finish, and know what every word is doing.

The text is Matthew 6:9–13 in the Cornilescu translation, the one you are most likely to hear in a Romanian Baptist church.

Read it aloud

One line at a time. Read first, in a whisper if the house is asleep. Open Sound and sense only after you've tried.

Tatăl nostru

About the capitals. The sounding-out lines are rough English approximations by the author. Use them to check a guess. The Romanian spelling is the better guide, and now you can read it.

One line you haven't seen

The prayer closes with a sentence this course has not shown you. Decode it cold. You know Împărăția, și and în already.

Check yourself

kuhch ah tah YES-teh uhm-puh-ruh-TSEE-ah shee poo-TEH-reh-ah shee SLAH-vah uhn vech. ah-MEEN.

For Yours is the Kingdom and the power and the glory forever. Amen.

The whispered pair is Căci and veci. The hidden “y” is in este.

Checkpoint drill

Ten questions across the whole week. Eight or better and week one is done.

Week one checkpoint

What you can do now

  • Sound out any Romanian word from its spelling.
  • Tell ă, â, î, ș and ț apart at a glance, in old spelling or new.
  • Recognise a few hundred Latin-family words on sight.
  • Read one passage of Scripture with every word accounted for.

Open the reader and mark the words of the prayer you now know. Next week starts on whatever is left dotted.

On Sunday, listen for și. You will hear it within ten seconds, and then about four hundred more times.
