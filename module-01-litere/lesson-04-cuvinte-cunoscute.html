Sixty words you already know

A day off from rules. Collect the vocabulary English and Romanian both took from Latin.

25 minNo audio neededDrill inside

Three days of letters. Today you collect the reward: a few hundred Romanian words that you can already read, because you already know them.

Romanian is a Romance language, descended from Latin alongside French, Spanish and Italian. English took a huge share of its formal vocabulary from the same place, and the lookalikes mostly share a Latin origin, often passed along through French.

Why this matters for a sermon. Preaching leans on abstract words: grace, authority, eternity, generation. Those are exactly the words that travel. The short everyday words are the hard ones, and week two is for them.

Reading them properly

Looking familiar and sounding familiar are different things. general has a soft g: jeh-neh-RAHL. lecție is LEK-tsee-eh. religios is reh-lee-jee-OS. Run the rules; don't read them as English.

The ones that lie

A few lookalikes kept an older meaning while English drifted. Romanian sensibil means “sensitive”, and eventual means “possible”, never “final”.

Guess the meaning, then tap

A librărie sells books and will not lend you one. The lending happens at the bibliotecă.
