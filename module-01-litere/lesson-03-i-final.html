The whispered final -i, and where the stress lands

The last quirk of Romanian spelling, two glides, and a relaxed approach to stress.

25 minNo audio neededDrill inside

One letter is left that doesn't always say its name: an i at the very end of a word. It matters more than it sounds, because it marks most masculine plurals and the “you” form of nearly every verb.

The whisper

When a word ends in a consonant plus i, that i is usually not pronounced as a vowel. It leaves a trace on the consonant before it, a faint “y” that never becomes a syllable. One description calls it whispered to near-silence.

Guess the sound, then tap

Write a tiny raised “y” after each of those in your head. One syllable for frați, never two.

When the i is a full “ee”

  • It is the only vowel. și (and), zi (day), ci (but rather).
  • It is doubled. A final i is silent unless another i comes before it: copii, children, is ko-PEE.
  • It carries the stress. Verbs in their dictionary form: a veni (to come), a citi (to read), a iubi (to love).
  • The consonants before it need help. After a cluster ending in r or l, the mouth needs a real vowel: noștri, our, is NOSH-tree.

Copiii has three in a row and means “the children”. It is a real word, and Romanian children find it as funny as you do.

Two glides worth knowing

A good reading routine checks for the ea and oa glides and a short list of e-glide words after the letters you already know.

ea and oa are one beat

ea slides like “ya” and oa like “wa”. So mea (my) is “mya”, toate (all) is TWAH-teh, and noastră (our) is NWAHS-truh.

A few words start with a hidden “y”

The commonest little words beginning with e are said with a y-glide in front: el (he) is “yel”, este (is) is YES-teh, ești (you are) is “yesht”.

Where the stress lands

Romanian doesn't write stress, and there is no single rule that predicts it. These three habits are the author's working guesses, and they are right often enough to read with:

  1. Strip the glued-on “the” first. Stress stays where it was: nume is NOO-meh, so numele is NOO-meh-leh.
  2. Ends in a consonant? Try the last syllable. pământ puh-MUHNT, cuvânt koo-VUHNT (word).
  3. Ends in a vowel? Try the one before. casă KAH-suh, pâine PUY-neh.

Don't chase it. A misplaced stress still gets you understood, and week six fixes it by ear. On the page it costs you nothing.
