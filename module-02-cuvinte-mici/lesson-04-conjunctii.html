And, but, because, therefore

The words that carry an argument. Follow these and you can follow a sermon whose nouns you only half know.

25 minNo audio neededDrill inside

A sermon is an argument, and arguments run on connectives. Catch pentru că and you know a reason is coming. Catch de aceea and you know the conclusion has arrived.

The quotations are from 1 Corinthians 13, Psalm 23, John 3, Matthew 7 and Luke 15.

Two kinds of “but”

dar is the ordinary one. ci is used only after a negative, to replace what was just denied: one dictionary's example is “not with two i's, ci with one”. You met it in the prayer: nu ne duce în ispită, ci izbăvește-ne.

Three near-twins. că is “that”. ca is “as” or “like”. căci is “for”. The marks are doing real work here.

pentru că is “because”. pentru ca is “so that”. One small mark separates a reason from a purpose, which is also true of most excuses.
