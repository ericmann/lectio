Week 2 · Lesson 6

Checkpoint: read a church bulletin

A week of small words, put to work on the kind of page handed out at the door.

25 minNo audio neededCheckpoint

Below is a Sunday bulletin written for this lesson. It is practice text, and no church published it. The service times echo the 9:00 and 6:00 Sunday gatherings your church lists.

Read it

Read all eight lines before you open anything. Then say, in English, what happens at each time.

Check yourself

Romanian Baptist Church · Sunday's schedule · 9:00 prayer and singing · 10:00 Bible study for children and young people · 10:30 the sermon, “The Lord is my Shepherd” · 6:00 the evening service · Thursday at 7:00, an evening of prayer · We look forward to seeing you.

Vă așteptăm cu drag is literally “we await you with fondness”.

Open it in the reader

Checkpoint drill

Eight or better and week two is done.

Week two checkpoint

What you can do now

  • Tell who is acting from a verb ending, without a pronoun.
  • Peel “the” and “of the” off the end of a noun.
  • Follow the logic of a sentence from its connectives.
  • Read a time, a day and a chapter-and-verse reference.

You can now read the bulletin. Reading the announcements slide before it changes remains an advanced skill in any language.
